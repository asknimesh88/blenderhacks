BlenderHacks is a site about **kitchen blenders**: how to use them better, keep them clean, fix common annoyances, and choose one that fits the way you cook.

## How we write our articles

- **Tips and how-tos** are based on how blenders work. They're written to apply to most machines, and we point out when a tip only fits certain types (such as high-performance or personal blenders).
- **Buying advice is research-based.** We haven't tested every blender we mention. We compare manufacturer specifications, warranty terms, and long-term feedback from owners, and we say so plainly. If we've used a product ourselves, the article will tell you.
- **We don't publish prices** because they change constantly. Follow the link to see the current price.
- **Always follow your blender's manual**, especially for hot liquids, cleaning, and anything involving the blade assembly.
